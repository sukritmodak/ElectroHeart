DEVICE SETUP

Connect your ElectroHeart

Pair your ESP32 over Bluetooth Low Energy and start viewing live acoustic signals.

Not connected
Received frames: 0

Direct BLE connection to ESP32-WROOM. The Bluetooth chooser shows nearby BLE devices; select ElectroHeart-BLE (or the ESP32 device name). The dashboard verifies the ElectroHeart service after connection. The device sends two 8-byte sample frames per 16-byte BLE notification. This deliberately fits the default BLE ATT payload, avoiding dependence on successful MTU negotiation. Use the AO (analog output) pin, not DO (digital threshold output). Power the modules from 3.3 V, connect grounds together, and keep AO within 0–3.3 V. The dashboard preserves raw ADC samples for plotting and recording; a separate baseline-corrected copy is used for display analysis and estimated beat-candidate timing. No USB data connection, COM port, or Python bridge is required. Use Chrome or Edge over HTTPS.

Bluetooth data is sent directly from the ESP32 over BLE. Connect each microphone AO pin to its own ESP32 ADC input (GPIO 34, 35, 32). Power modules from 3.3 V, share GND, and keep AO within 0–3.3 V. The dashboard tracks each module's DC bias automatically.
