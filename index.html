Connect your ElectroHeart

Pair your ESP32 over Bluetooth Low Energy and start viewing live acoustic signals.

Direct BLE connection to ESP32-WROOM. The Bluetooth chooser shows nearby BLE devices; select ElectroHeart-BLE (or the ESP32 device name). The dashboard verifies the ElectroHeart service after connection. The device sends batches of binary PCG samples through a BLE notification characteristic. Use the AO (analog output) pin, not DO (digital threshold output). Power the modules from 3.3 V, connect grounds together, and keep AO within 0–3.3 V. The dashboard automatically removes each microphone's DC offset before plotting, event detection, and audio playback. No USB data connection, COM port, or Python bridge is required. Use Chrome or Edge over HTTPS.

Experimental acoustic event detector. S1 is paired to a following S2 when the interval is 100–500 ms. Heart rate is calculated from successive S1-to-S1 intervals, never from S1–S2 spacing. BPM remains blank until valid cycles are observed; this is a research prototype, not a diagnostic monitor.

Bluetooth data is sent directly from the ESP32 over BLE. Connect each microphone AO pin to its own ESP32 ADC input (GPIO 34, 35, 32). Power modules from 3.3 V, share GND, and keep AO within 0–3.3 V. The dashboard tracks each module's DC bias automatically.
